## Definition of a definition

a statement about a thing, that contains specific key characteristics

### Examples

- AI -- computer systems and machines built to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence
- Smartphone -- a device that can perform phone calls, access internet and run other software (e.g games)

### Common mistakes

- confusing description with a definition
- overspecification
- not giving a concept
- circular definition
